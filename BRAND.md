# Attention Span — visual identity brief

## Big idea: the cat runs the dot

Everyone expects the ADHD cat to be the distractible one, chasing the laser. We flip it. **The cat holds the pointer.** It decides where the dot goes, and it lands the dot on the one line that matters. Focus was never about killing the laser, it's about who's holding it. The cat aims your attention *for* you, playfully, never scolding. That's the ally.

## Why it's not a cliché

The theme is **not** "chaos to clarity." ADHD attention isn't chaos, it's democracy: everything competes at equal loudness. Focus is letting one thing raise its hand. So the visual is **"everything shouting equally → one line lit,"** which is a literal demo of what the output style does (greys the noise, lights the bold), not a tidy-up metaphor.

## Brand mechanics

- **The dot = brand orange `#e8590c`**, the same color as the `→` markers in the styles. The laser dot and the arrow are one beam of attention.
- **Before/after** = the cat sweeps the dot across a wall of equal-grey text; wherever it lands, that line lights up bold. Same content, the cat chooses.
- **Tagline** "pay attention, not tokens" — the cat spends the dot wisely.

## Mascot personality

Calm, competent, a little smug in a lovable way. The opposite of frazzled. An ADHD ally who's got the remote. Never a scold.

## Assets to make

1. **Hero mascot** (GPT image): the cat, relaxed, one paw resting on a laser pointer, an orange dot beaming onto a single glowing line inside a wall of dim grey text. Editorial, warm, works on a light background.
2. **Favicon / sticker** (GPT image): just the cat's face, a tiny orange dot reflected in one eye. Simple enough to read at 32px.
3. **Before/after strip** (`/illo` explainer diagram): the sweep. Grey wall of text → dot lands → one line lit. No mascot needed.

## Palette

- Ink: `#1a1c1f`
- Paper: `#f6f7f9` / `#ffffff`
- Muted grey (the "noise"): `#adb5bd`
